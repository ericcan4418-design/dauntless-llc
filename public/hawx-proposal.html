The Problem Hawx Has Right Now

Every summer, Hawx invests heavily in recruiting door-to-door sales reps. But three things quietly kill ROI every season:

  • Bad leads getting through. Unqualified reps make it to training, waste manager time, and quit week two.
  • Slow onboarding kills momentum. New reps are left to figure out the platform, the pitch, and the process on their own. The longer it takes to get their first sale, the higher the chance they don't come back.
  • Managers have no visibility. There's no single place to see which reps are active, stuck, ghosting, or crushing it — until it's too late to do anything about it.

The result: High churn, high recruiting costs, undertrained reps in the field, and managers spending more time chasing people than leading them.

What AMPA Is Building

AMPA is a full-stack recruiting and rep management system designed specifically for the D2D industry. It handles everything from the moment a rep sees an ad to the moment they're producing in the field — and everything in between.

This is not a generic CRM. This is built ground-up for how D2D sales teams actually operate.

The Full System — Point A to Point Z

€�; Point A — Recruiting Funnel

A high-converting landing page targeted at 18-22 year olds looking for high-income opportunities in pest control, solar, fiber, and insurance.

  • Attracts candidates via Meta/Instagram ads
  • Qualifies intent with a short application form (state, vertical, experience, travel availability)
  • Captures explicit SMS consent — fully TCPA and A2P 10DLC compliant
  • Immediately triggers the AI screener the moment someone submits

Why it matters for Hawx: Only serious, pre-qualified candidates ever reach your managers.

🤖 Point B — Sarah (AI Voice Screener)

An AI phone recruiter named Sarah that calls every applicant within 15 seconds of form submission — 24/7, no manager required.

  • Discloses she's an AI, asks permission to continue
  • Hard-screens for 4 disqualifiers: commission-only, long hours, travel, no employer insurance
  • Runs a structured qualifying interview: why sales, goals, 1-10 interest score, start date
  • Qualified leads get a mystery close that builds curiosity for the M2 conversation
  • Full transcript, outcome, structured data, and recording logged automatically

Tech: Vapi.ai + ElevenLabs Flash v2.5 + Deepgram Nova-3 + GPT-4o-mini. Sub-2 second response latency.

Why it matters for Hawx: Managers only spend time on leads already screened, motivated, and primed. Sarah runs while they sleep.

🧠 Point D — William (AI Onboarding Bot)

An AI onboarding assistant named William that lives inside the AMPA Network platform and walks every new rep through onboarding step by step — from day one to first Blitz.

Rookie Flow

  • Get set up on payroll (guided walkthrough + contact for issues)
  • Join the team group chat + introduce yourself
  • Complete training modules in the correct order
  • Memorize the pitch (William coaches step by step)
  • Schedule and pass the manager pass-off meeting
  • Schedule first Blitz — 1-2 week field simulation

Veteran Flow

  • Walk through the leadership course (hire, fire, interview, recruit)
  • Get integrated into management structure
  • Vets who just want to sell: train, join the chat, get on Blitzes

Key behavior: If a rep hasn't moved to the next step in 48 hours, William follows up once — smart, not spammy.

Why it matters for Hawx: A rep who gets their first sale within 30 days is dramatically more likely to finish the season. William's entire job is to compress that timeline.

💻 Point E — Rep Management Command Center

A standalone management platform that syncs from AMPA Network and gives every recruiter and manager a real-time view of their entire downline.

  • Every rep signed on AMPA Network auto-populates into the dashboard
  • 🟢 Green — fully onboarded, active, producing
  • 🟡 Yellow — onboarding in progress, steps incomplete
  • 🔴 Red — ghost mode, no engagement in X days
  • Built-in SMS and calling — reach any rep directly from the dashboard
  • Progress timeline — see exactly where each rep is stuck
  • Phase 2: Live revenue integration — Hawx sales data next to every rep's profile
  • Field support — send reps scripts, resources, and coaching in real time while they're knocking

Why it matters for Hawx: Right now, managers find out a rep quit when they stop showing up. This platform shows the warning signs weeks earlier — giving managers a real chance to intervene.

📊 Point F — Commission Dashboard

A live earnings tracker showing every rep exactly where they stand — built on Hawx's actual pay scales.

  • Real-time commission calculation at every account milestone
  • Rookie and veteran pay scale tiers built in (26% up to 75% Golden Door)
  • Shows reps exactly how many accounts to the next tier
  • Golden Door target always visible — motivation baked in

Why it matters for Hawx: Money is the #1 motivator in D2D. When reps can see their exact number in real time, they knock harder.
