# dauntless-llc

**Live:** [dauntless-llc.vercel.app](https://dauntless-llc.vercel.app)

Corporate brand site for Dauntless LLC — the legal operating entity behind AMPA Recruiting.

---

## Why This Exists

Primarily used for **A2P 10DLC carrier registration** — so that SMS texts to candidates come from "Dauntless" as a verified business, not an unregistered number. Carriers require a legitimate business website with a privacy policy and terms of service before approving SMS campaigns.

---

## Pages

| Route | What It Is |
|-------|-----------|
| `/` | Homepage — "Building America's Top Sales Teams" |
| `/privacy` | Full Privacy Policy (TCPA/SMS compliant, names Dauntless LLC) |
| `/terms` | Terms of Service (SMS consent language) |
